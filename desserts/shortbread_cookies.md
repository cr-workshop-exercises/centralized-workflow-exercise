# Shortbread cookies

## Ingredients

- butter
- flour
- creme fraiche
- egg yolk
- sugar

## Instructions

- mix sugar with cold butter
- add slowly the flour and mix until getting a sand texture
- add egg yolk and creme fraiche
- knead the dough until uniform and let it rest in the frige for 20 min
- extend dough and cut in your favourite shape
- bake at 180 for 15 min
