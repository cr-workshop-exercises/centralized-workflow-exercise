# Bananabread

## Ingredients
- 3 Ripe bananas
- 80 ml Neutral oil
- 90 - 110 g Brown sugar (Depending on how ripe the bananas are, less sugar is possible)
- 2 eggs
- 200g of wheat flour
- 3 TS baking powder
- Salt (Don't overdo, does not taste well)
- Vanilla
- Cinnamon

## Instructions
- Preheat Oven (1800°C). put baking-paper into the baking form. Squish bananas in a bowl with fork
- Mix oil with brown sugar and eggs. Mix flour, baking powder, salt, cinnamon and vanilla, add the oil mixture and slowly add bananas. Pour mixture into the baking form and bake in the oven for around 55 minutes and let cool completely before consumption.
