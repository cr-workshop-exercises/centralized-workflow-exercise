# Pickles

## Ingredients:

- 2–3 cucumbers (unpeeled, but sliced or spears)
- 1 cup water
- 1 cup white vinegar
- 1 cup salt
- 1 tbsp sugar
- 2–3 garlic cloves (optional)
- 1 tsp peppercorns
- Fresh dill (optional)

## Instructions:

Pack sliced cucumbers into a clean jar. Add garlic, dill, and peppercorns.
In a small pot, heat water + vinegar + salt + sugar until dissolved (don’t boil).
Pour the warm brine over the cucumbers until fully covered.
Let cool, then refrigerate at least 2 hours (best after 24 hours).
