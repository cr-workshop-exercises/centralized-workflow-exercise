# Chicken Tenders

## Ingredients

- 500 g chicken breast
- 1 cup breadcrumbs
- 1/2 cup flour
- 2 eggs
- 1 teaspoon salt
- 1/2 teaspoon black pepper
- 1 teaspoon paprika
- 1/2 teaspoon garlic powder
- Cooking oil

## Instructions

1. Cut the chicken breast into strips.
2. Put the flour in one bowl.
3. Beat the eggs in a second bowl.
4. Mix the breadcrumbs, salt, black pepper, paprika, and garlic powder in a third bowl.
5. Coat each chicken strip in flour, then dip it in the egg, and finally coat it with the breadcrumb mixture.
6. Heat some oil in a frying pan over medium heat.
7. Fry the chicken tenders for about 4-5 minutes on each side until golden brown and fully cooked.
8. Serve warm with your favorite dipping sauce.
