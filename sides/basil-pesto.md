# Basil pesto

## Ingredients
- 1 basil plant
- 20 g cashews or pine nuts (toasted in a dry pan if you like)
- 25 g parmesan
- 1 dl olive oil
- 1 tsp lemon juice
- Salt and pepper

## Instructions
- Mix everything together with an immersion blender or a small food processor/blender.
- Store in the refrigerator for up to one week.
