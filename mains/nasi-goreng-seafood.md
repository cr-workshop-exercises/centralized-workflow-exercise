## Seafood Fried Rice Recipe

## Ingredients:
- 1 plate cooked white rice
- 100 g seafood (shrimp, squid, or mix)
- 2 cloves garlic (minced)
- 1 egg
- 2 tbsp sweet soy sauce
- Salt & pepper to taste
- Cooking oil

## Instructions:
1. Heat oil, sauté garlic until fragrant, add seafood and cook through.
2. Push to the side, scramble the egg.
3. Add rice and mix well.
4. Season with soy sauce, salt, and pepper.
5. Stir until evenly heated, then serve.
